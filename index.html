CMYK
Light Reactive Art (as seen on the right) layers Cyan, Magenta, and Yellow to create a hidden collage with much to reveal.
Select a color below, drag the circle, and discover the hidden layers and images of CMYK.
Green reveals the magenta layer. Images of romance reflect the feelings of love that magenta represents.
The underwater cyan layer comes to life with red. Cyan is often associated with peace, which is replicated with the calm and peaceful imagery.
Yellow often highlights joy and fun, and blue brings the happiest layer into the forefront.
